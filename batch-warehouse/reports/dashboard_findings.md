# Dashboard findings

These are descriptive patterns in historical dispatch records. Counts are not population-adjusted, and differences do not establish causes. Response averages use only rows marked valid by the source and are shown with sample sizes. Borough comparisons below exclude missing/unknown borough values.

## Ten evidence-based findings

1. The warehouse contains 4,035,659 incidents in the selected January 2024–June 2026 period.
2. The busiest clock hour is 14:00–14:59, with 216,775 incidents.
3. Monday has the largest weekday total, at 592,381 incidents.
4. BROOKLYN has the most incidents: 1,114,292 (27.6% of all records).
5. RICHMOND / STATEN ISLAND has the smallest borough total: 173,379 (4.3%).
6. The highest borough average incident response is 21.0 minutes in BRONX (n=873,676 valid records).
7. The lowest borough average incident response is 10.4 minutes in RICHMOND / STATEN ISLAND (n=167,476 valid records).
8. INJURY is the most common initial call type, with 489,693 incidents (12.1%).
9. Among call types with at least 1,000 valid response records, STNDBY has the highest mean incident response (57.0 minutes; n=8,354).
10. Initial and final severity codes differ on 500,882 incidents (12.4%). This is a code transition, not a clinical interpretation.

## Additional observation

The busiest month is May 2024 (144,021 incidents).
